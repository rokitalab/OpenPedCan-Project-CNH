## Methylation mismatches

| Decision | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| A methylation mismatch is flagged only when the high-confidence class is completely mismatched: the genomics supports the diagnosis or another entity and not the class, or the class is mutually exclusive with a lesion (an IDH-mutant glioma class with an H3 K27 or G34 variant). `03-validate-subtypes.R` finds these from the lesions and the diagnosis, the reviewer decisions of `input/possible_methylation_mixups.tsv` (`likely`, with the evidence) take precedence, and every call is written to `results/methylation_mismatch_calls.tsv` (`call_source`, `found_from_lesions`). The arrays of a likely mismatch are probably from another specimen: no step uses them for the subtype (the tumor is subtyped from its lesions, a reviewer decision or the pathology diagnosis; a tumor with none of them has no row and keeps the frozen legacy label in the integrate step) and `results/methylation_likely_mismatch.tsv` lists them to be removed from the release. A methylation class that is only discordant with the diagnosis, with genomics that does not contradict it (no DNA or RNA, a low tumor purity so that a missing lesion means nothing, or no lesion either way), or whose genomics matches the class, is not a mismatch: the methylation subtype stays and the tumor carries the diagnosis-discordance flag (`review/methylation_diagnosis_discordance_notes.tsv` has the notes of 21 such tumors, previously tiered possible or kept). An EPN PF-A class with an H3 K27M variant is a recognised combination | the genomics and the diagnosis, not an a priori list, decide which methylation calls cannot be used; a missing lesion can be low tumor purity; the review of each new candidate is part of the review skill | `03-validate-subtypes.R`, `07-compile-subtypes.R`, `utils/mismatch-arrays.R`, `utils/check-subtyping.R` |
| Likely (8): 7316-166, -3402 and -7113 (CTNNB1 exon 3 hotspot in craniopharyngioma tumors called pilocytic astrocytoma), 7316-891 (KIAA1549::BRAF in the RNA, called MB SHH), 7316-3636 (ATRX and TP53 variants, no SMARCB1 loss, called ATRT SHH), 7316-3403 and -4323 (RNA only, SMARCB1 expressed, called ATRT SHH; weaker), 7316-1856 (Ewing sarcoma by pathology, called MB Group 4) | genomics or expression supports the diagnosis and not the methylation class | `input/possible_methylation_mixups.tsv` |
| The definitive-lesion contradiction in `03-validate-subtypes.R` only compares a methylation call with a lesion of the same family, so a craniopharyngioma CTNNB1 hotspot with a pilocytic astrocytoma call is not flagged by it (`lesion_status` is `no_rule`) | found while reviewing the discordant tumors; extending it across families is open | `03-validate-subtypes.R` |

## G-CIMP score and the ploidy-aware arm values

- G-CIMP is scored for every methylation array (one probe read, `08-score-gcimp.R`) but used only as evidence for IDH-mutant glioma classes (A_IDH_LG, A_IDH_HG, O_IDH); there is no cutoff or call, because the reference set (9 IDH-mutant arrays) is too small to set one. The score is the mean beta over the Noushmehr 2010 probes that are hypermethylated in the IDH-mutant arrays of this cohort.
- The ploidy-aware 1p/19q arm values (`02-prepare-lesion-tables.R`) count neutral segments (NA copy number in the consensus file) as the tumor ploidy and divide by the whole arm length; averaging only the called segments read focal losses as an arm loss (7316-6389 1p, and a false 1p+19q codeletion in three cell lines).

## Marker expression, mismatch flags and reproducibility

- Expression rules for marker genes of the four medulloblastoma groups are evidence only (`input/subtype_lesion_rules.tsv`, z >= 3 within library type; 16 genes). None fires in another group among 233 methylation-called tumors; 68-86% of a group has a marker. 7316-7965 (cell line): the expression classifier SHH call (p = 0.40) is weak and the markers fit Group 3, so the line is `MB, Group3` by a manual review (the parental tumor is Group 3 by methylation).
- `methylation and genomics agree, diagnosis differs` is a review flag (not a mismatch): the methylation subtype's own lesion is confirmed and the diagnosis is discordant, so the molecular data may not belong to the clinical record (7316-918, CHDM with an ATRT diagnosis).
- Reproducibility: the review tables are checked against the data on every run (orphans, empty notes, unknown labels, redundant or stale rows), and `results/run_provenance.tsv` records the inputs; a rerun on the same release must leave `git diff` of the results empty. A CI diff against the committed results is not possible: CI uses the small `testing` release and a standard runner cannot hold the 15 GB beta-value file.
